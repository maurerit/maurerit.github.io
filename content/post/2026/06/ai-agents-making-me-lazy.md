They say a good programmer is lazy... because we automate everything... but with AI... not only am I lazy but I feel like I'm learning less and have less knowledge on my app because I don't toil over the code.  The upside though is that I've been able to produce something in just 2 months that would have taken me a year by myself if not more.  I've been _trying_ to create this app for about 10 years now but I always hit a wall that I just don't have the time to handle.  Plus, this allows me to be the architect/tester/BA of the app and drop the coder role.  This is all of course for a hobby project so I'm using my spare time where I should be spending time with family.

I'm now able to kick an agent off and play chess with my kid or chat with my wife.  Something I still need to work on as I do still need to give the project some of my attention if I want to move it forward.  Things like checking what these agents do, testing the app, deciding what to implement and when to cut a release.  But I still feel... not quite right about the whole thing.  I manufacture more memories thinking I did something when it was done differently or not complete, losing state of things... kind of like how your manager is constantly losing track of what you're doing and you having to constantly remind them and bring them up to speed only for them to forget yet again unless the issue is critical enough that they actually got involved.

I also don't know how much of this is true.  Almost every issue is critical to me so I do get involved for the most part.  I review every line of code for at least common pit falls and standard practice deviations but I can't tell you anymore how a go channel works, a thing I learned 10 years ago when building my discord bot.  A project that took me like 3 months of constant work... and I do mean constant work.  I spent at least 6 hours a day for those three months grinding that thing, learning a language, learning a framework, deciding how to deploy, making sure my local instance is running properly and don't even get me started on keeping it current... The last one I'll let you know how things go once I get dependabot plugged into this project somehow... which means... putting it up on github and enabling dependabot and then finding a way to separate that .github folder from the main source tree so that gitea doesn't try and run anything in it... such a hassle in order to use some tech to accomplish a goal...

But really the topic is about how AI is making me lazy... maybe it's actually not making me lazy but it IS turning me into a manager of sorts... a good thing?  Probably, because quite frankly, I'm tired of just laying down code, I want something more, I want to get involved at a deeper level.  I can throw out proofs faster and let what sticks to the wall stick to the wall instead of meticuously boiling it and while that's happening sticking them to the wall to see if it sticks... now I can generate proof, throw at wall... rinse repeat.  We'll see how long that lasts...
