Good job boomers, way to look out for those that came after you.  The boomer generation makes me sick, this also applies to older Gen X'ers. They were out protesting nuclear constructions even though we NEEDED the power density then... now more so than ever... uneducated fools...

What tripped me onto this, and I've done this analysis several times over, is the sickening profits that big tech rakes in all while firing people for higher profits.  Fuck the shareholder, they did fuck all to build the company and keep it running, they should be left to their own devices and forced to sell to make their money.  Dividends... maybe a little bit but not much at all.  Want to reap the benefits of the company... go work for it instead.  Today I just did a quick analysis "what if" if the likes of Meta, Microsoft, Google and Apple distributed HALF of their profits to each of their employee's equally... the numbers... they're sickening... all 4 of these companies could literally pay top dollar for their employee's by paying them ANOTHER year of salary, as a top earner, if they got a bonus with half of the companies profits.  Seems fair to me... without the workers... you have NOTHING.

It's time to rise up and take back what's ours.  We just demand a little more respect and compensation for our labors.
