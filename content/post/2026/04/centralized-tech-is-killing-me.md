Over the past couple of months you've seen me setup some stuff to start taking back my personal data.  I'm still making some of it public but by in large I'm back in control of my data.  There are still going to forever be holdouts... communities I like on discord that just won't move because it's too convenient and too much content already lives there, yada yada.  What we don't always realize is that for the most part the majority of these mainstream 'communities' are just echo chambers with very little actual good content to be consumed.

You join a community because you like the game developer... then you realize there's thousands of other people with the same feelings... then you realize that you know none of them and they're all just 'screaming' in this echo chamber producing garbage you have to sift through to get to the content you want.  If you do find any connections in these communities you may spin off and talk in dm's or start a community... but where do you do it?  On the centralized platform...  Sure, we get 'private spaces' on these platforms and they really want to make you think that so that you produce more data and complete their profile of you.  Social graphs, likes and dislikes, sites visited, twitter handles, steam handles, xbox handles, twitch handles... any data they can vacuum and use to make money.

Think about that some more while you're here and throughout your days moving forward.  Reflect on why you use those platforms instead of others that are out there.  Is it ignorance of the other platforms?  Is it because they're too obtuse with their actual uses.  They just focus on the technical bits which you get working but then you see nothing... blank slate... and you know not what to do... Do you seek help?  Did you try leaving Google to get the information you're seeking?  Do you listen to any tech savy people around you?  Have you fired up a privacy conversation with the tech savy people around you instead of asking them for tech support?  They might actually not roll their eyes at you if you start a genuine conversation on data sovereignty and how you can take control back.  But who am I talking to... if you're on these pages then you're more than likely entrenched in a much deeper internet than others.

So why am I writing this post... well... a couple of things...  Firstly, my tech [did weird things to me today](https://social.r3r4um.net/objects/019d816c-74a9-6275-00c6-e5826c9a08e2) and my son is already so indoctrinated into the 'mainstream' that I fear it's too late to convince him that he needs to make a different decision... and yes I know... I'm his father but he's also his own person... a balance we're striking... but I digress... I want him to see about convincing his friends to use MY services instead of for instance Discord (which I fought for a couple of years at least).  I have a perfectly acceptable solution that would keep him less exposed to the craziness of the internet but still offer him voice and video chat plus screen sharing.

I have gotten him to create an account, maybe he'll get bored one day and go play around with it and convince his buddies to goof around with it.  One can only hope.  Now I just gotta make it more reliable and not let the database server go down :D.
