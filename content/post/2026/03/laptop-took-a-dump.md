I was out in the living room enjoying my computing situation that I've recently been afforded and goofing off with my son when my laptop all of a sudden starts shutting down.  I think nothing of it because my son was mashing buttons like a wild monkey and I figure he hit the power button.  So I harass him some more while the laptop shuts down and afterwards I try to power it on and nothing... try again... nothing... Ok, I don't mess up the press twice in a row, something is up.  The battery last I remember was around 80% and nowhere near being shutdown worthy.

So I take the laptop back into the office and remove the back to get to the battery.  Fiddle with the connector for a few minutes while I figure out how it works without breaking it and then plug it into the wall to see what's up.  Charge indicator comes on but is blinking... ok... decent sign of life so I hit the power button and it starts to power on.  Good, that part is probably mostly alive.

Not wanting it to go through whatever self check it does after being completely powered off I shut it down and unplug it.  Plug the battery back in and attempt to power on.  Nothing, plug it in, nothing.  So I think "ok then... I'm now in possession of an all in one desktop machine"...  So I rip the battery out all the way and just settle for it being stuck in the office during this upcoming hot months with no central air :D.  Yippee.

I'm attempting to see if I can get it repaired but my hopes are low because it's a laptop I bought off of what appears like an outlet dealer.  I put the serial number into ASUS and they told me to go to Best Buy to get my potential warranty service.  I don't much like Best Buy anymore and don't feel like stripping my drives out and taking it in.  I might just settle for this being a desktop machine and put it into the cluster.  Time will tell and my persistence, lacking as it is, will determine if I RMA/Repair this thing.

For the potential future I'm eye balling a Macbook Neo for my living room computing.  While I was enjoying my time out in the living room I was also still tethered to the wall due to the dedicated gpu that I don't even use out there.  A Macbook Neo is cheap enough to be considered for light work, repairable and still of a decent quality.  Go figure, Apple finally comes into the right way of offering hardware.

For that potential future, I'm looking at finding a light weight browser, maybe a firefox fork or firefox itself?  But I need two browsers, one for browsing i2p and one for browsing the clear net.  There should also already be a good fediverse native client so we'll run that.  I'll run discord, signal, telegram and element x... jeeze that's just crazy.  My current workload on this laptop right now is 11.9gb of memory.  That workload matches what I just said.  If they dedicate half the memory to compressed memory that allows for 12gb of usable memory.  That should work well for this workload.  I can always close some things as well.  Discord and Telegram will need opened.  Signal can be used on the phone.  Element is slow enough I can just check it on the phone periodically.  And I'll have mitra opened in a browser tab.  VS Code should also fit in memory since I'm running 2 instances of it now.

This might just work, into the future!
